Good To Know Before You Ride

These answers reflect information currently published by Sacred Spur for the 2026 Highland Park Christmas carriage season.

How long is a carriage ride?

Every Sacred Spur carriage ride is a 60-minute private experience through Highland Park's celebrated Christmas-light streets.

How much does a Sacred Spur carriage tour cost?

Pricing varies by carriage size. The available rate, applicable taxes, and Xola's 6% booking fee are displayed during checkout before payment.

Is the carriage private, or shared with other guests?

Every tour is reserved by the carriage, not the seat — your party rides alone, with no strangers sharing the bench.

What should I wear?

Dress for the outdoors — evenings in Highland Park can be cool. Lap blankets are provided on every carriage.

How many people can ride together?

Carriages range from an intimate 4-passenger Vis-à-Vis up to a 12-passenger Limo Carriage for larger families and groups.

How far in advance should I book?

Evenings are limited to a small number of departures each night, so we recommend reserving several weeks ahead for peak December dates.

Where do tours depart from?

Carriage rides meet at 4711 Westside Drive in Dallas. Parking and check-in details are provided with your booking confirmation.

Are rides available on Thanksgiving Day?

No, we’ll be closed on Thanksgiving Day. We start the day after Thanksgiving and operate seven days a week, including Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Rides continue past Christmas.

What happens if weather affects a ride?

We may call a rain delay due to inclement weather and/or for the safety of the horses. Your ride will be rescheduled or refunded, based on availability.

What is the cancellation policy?

You may cancel up to 48 hours before your ride. Cancellations are not available within 48 hours of your scheduled ride.

Can I give a carriage ride as a gift?

Yes! During checkout, visit the gift certificate section to purchase a carriage ride certificate for anyone you choose. Groups and organizations are welcome to purchase rides to gift to a special friend or family—or to someone in need.

Are reservations required?

Yes. Sacred Spur requires reservations for Highland Park Christmas carriage rides.

Where can I see current availability?

Use the Book Now button to open Xola, where current carriage availability, departure times, pricing, taxes, and booking fees are shown before payment.

We look forward to seeing you for a holiday ride with Sacred Spur Carriage Company.

Need An Answer That Is Not Listed?

For reservation-specific questions or additional details, please contact Sacred Spur directly.
