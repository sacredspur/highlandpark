Before You Ride

Know What To Expect

The homepage introduces the experience. This page is the practical guide: what the ride includes, how the evening flows, what to review after booking, and where to go when it is time to meet your carriage.

I.

Reserve Your Carriage

Choose from 4-, 6-, 8-, or 12-passenger options. Reservations are required, and peak December dates can fill several weeks ahead.

II.

Private For Your Party

Reservations are by the carriage, not by the seat. Your group rides together without unrelated guests sharing the carriage.

III.

60 Minutes

Every Sacred Spur Highland Park Christmas carriage experience lasts one hour.

IV.

The Highland Park Route

The experience follows celebrated Highland Park streets and holiday displays, with the route refined around the season and neighborhood conditions.

V.

Comfort Along The Way

Dress for the outdoors. Highland Park evenings can be cool, and lap blankets are provided on every carriage.

VI.

Review Your Confirmation

After booking through Xola, review your confirmation for reservation and check-in details before heading to the meeting area.

The Experience

Highland Park At An Unhurried Pace

The carriage experience is designed around the neighborhood's Christmas displays, including the atmosphere of streets such as Armstrong Parkway, Beverly Drive, and Preston Road.

I.

Well-Mannered Horses

Sacred Spur's carriage horses are road-seasoned and accustomed to the quiet streets of the Park Cities.

II.

Experienced Drivers

Your driver handles the route and the horses so your party can focus on the lights and the evening together.

III.

A Private Departure

The carriage is reserved for your party, giving the ride a quieter and more personal feel.

Planning note: seasonal traffic and lighting conditions can change throughout December. Your booking confirmation is the best place to check reservation-specific information before departure.
