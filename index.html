The Season, By Carriage

Each December, the estates of Highland Park transform into one of the most celebrated holiday displays in Texas — candlelit walkways, wreathed iron gates, and grand illuminated facades along Armstrong Parkway, Beverly Drive, and Preston Road. Sacred Spur Carriage Company offers a quieter, more elegant way to take it in: a private carriage, a gentle pair of horses, and an unhurried pace through the Park Cities' most storied streets.

Reservations are required. Evenings are limited to a small number of departures each night to preserve the pace and privacy of every tour. We recommend booking several weeks ahead for peak December dates.

Questions before you reserve? Call us at 903-790-2693.

What To Expect

Every detail of a Sacred Spur evening is considered, from the route we choose to the horses that walk it.

I.

The Highland Park Route

A curated path through the neighborhood's most celebrated blocks, timed to the season's lighting displays and refined each year with the streets in mind.

II.

Well-Mannered Horses

Our carriage horses are gentle, road-seasoned, and accustomed to the quiet, tree-lined streets of the Park Cities — a calm, steady ride from start to finish.

III.

A Private Departure

Tours are reserved by the carriage, not the seat — your party rides alone, with no strangers sharing the bench.

IV.

White-Glove Service

From lap blankets to lantern light, every detail is attended to by an experienced driver, ensuring the evening feels as considered as the destination.

V.

A Knowledgeable Driver

Our drivers know the neighborhood's history as well as its lights, and are glad to share both along the way.

VI.

An Evening to Remember

Ideal for anniversaries, proposals, family traditions, and quiet evenings — a Sacred Spur tour is built to become an annual ritual.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a carriage ride?

Tours run in 30 and 60-minute routes through Highland Park's most celebrated Christmas-light streets, including Armstrong Parkway and Beverly Drive.

How much does a Sacred Spur carriage tour cost?

Pricing varies by carriage size and tour length. Exact rates for each carriage are shown when you check availability and book.

Is the carriage private, or shared with other guests?

Every tour is reserved by the carriage, not the seat — your party rides alone, with no strangers sharing the bench.

What should I wear?

Dress for the outdoors — evenings in Highland Park can be cool. Lap blankets are provided on every carriage.

How many people can ride together?

Carriages range from an intimate 4-passenger Vis-à-Vis up to a 12-passenger Limo Carriage for larger families and groups.

How far in advance should I book?

Evenings are limited to a small number of departures each night, so we recommend reserving several weeks ahead for peak December dates.

Where do tours depart from?

Departure details and the exact meeting point are provided with your booking confirmation.
